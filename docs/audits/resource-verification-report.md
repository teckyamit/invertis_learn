## 1. Quality Scoring Rubric

All 79 candidate resources were evaluated across a 100-point academic quality framework:
- **Curriculum Coverage (35 pts):** Alignment with Invertis University unit topics and syllabus breadth.
- **Provider Credibility (20 pts):** Accredited university, premier national initiative (NPTEL, MIT, Stanford), or recognized educator (GATE Smashers, Knowledge Gate).
- **Availability & Stability (20 pts):** Publicly accessible without paywall, registration barrier, or private status.
- **Pedagogical Clarity (10 pts):** Structured sequencing, clear audio/video production, or textbook diagram quality.
- **Academic Rigor (10 pts):** Formal theoretical proofs, algorithm complexities, or standard engineering conventions.
- **Platform Integrity (5 pts):** Ad-free or minimal distractions; no malware/tracker risk.

## 4. Playback & Security Policy

In accordance with user specifications:
1. **Zero embedded iframes:** Embedded YouTube video players have been completely eradicated from the codebase.
2. **External Link Protocol:** All video and note links render with `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
3. **Student Reporting Modal:** Interactive `ReportContentModal` enables students to flag any dead external links directly to the curator dashboard.
