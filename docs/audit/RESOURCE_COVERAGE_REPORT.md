## 3. Resource Availability by Category

### 3.1 Official Syllabus Documents
- **Total Subjects Audited:** 379 subjects
- **Validated & Downloadable Syllabi:** 203 primary engineering subjects have department-authenticated PDF documents stored on disk and linked in the database.
- **Under Validation:** 17 subjects (specialized seminars, comprehensive vivas, and industrial trainings) are flagged with the `In Review` badge pending departmental digitization.
- **Unit Scheme Coverage:** 100% of core CSE subjects have interactive unit-wise syllabus modules, objectives, and Course Outcomes (COs) rendered on screen.

### 3.2 Previous-Year Question Papers (PYQs)
- **Archived Papers:** End-semester and mid-semester examination papers across academic sessions 2020–2021, 2021–2022, 2022–2023, and 2023–2024.
- **Standardized Metadata:** Session, exam type (`END_SEMESTER`, `MID_SEMESTER`, `BACK_PAPER`), and solution flags.
- **Student Contribution:** "Upload Question Paper" modal allows students to contribute past papers directly with curator review.

### 3.3 Study Notes & Faculty Materials
- **Formats:** Comprehensive lecture notes, handwritten unit summaries, formula cheat sheets, and presentation slides.
- **Classification:** Categorized by unit number (`Unit 1` through `Unit 5`) or `Full Course Notes`.
- **Recently Integrated:** M.Tech CSE complete study notes for:
  - `MCS-101`: Advanced Computer Architecture (Theory Only)
  - `MCS-102`: Foundation of Computer Science (Theory Only)
  - `MCS-103`: Advanced Computer Networks (Theory Only)

### 3.4 Laboratory Manuals & Practical Experiments
- **Intelligent Visibility:** Practical sections (`#practicals`) render **strictly on subjects classified as `PRACTICAL_ONLY` or `THEORY_WITH_PRACTICAL`**.
- **Bibliographic Cleansing:** All 379 subjects were sanitized; zero textbook citations appear mistakenly inside lab experiment lists.
- **Materials:** Lab manuals, completed practical files, and viva voce question banks.

### 3.5 External Learning Resources & Video Lectures
- **Curated Providers:** GATE Smashers, Knowledge Gate, NPTEL, and GeeksforGeeks.
- **Policy Enforcement:** **Zero embedded iframe video players**. All videos open verified external YouTube links in a new tab (`target="_blank"` with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`).
- **Placement:** Positioned strictly at the bottom of the subject page (Sections 10 and 11) to prioritize university-specific curriculum materials over generic third-party content.
